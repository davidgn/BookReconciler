# Global Authority Catalog (OpenRefine Reconciliation Service)

This document maps the 5,938 registered reconciliation services to their actual underlying bibliographic protocols.
## Tier 1: National Bibliographic Authorities
* **SRU (Standard)**: Primary hubs using standard CQL-based SRU.
* **REST/API**: Modern web-native hubs (Zenodo, arXiv, OECD, WTO).
## Tier 2: Subnational Authorities (State/Provincial)
* **Alma SRU Nodes**: 100% of US/Canadian state/provincial libraries registered.
* **AbsysNet/Sebina Nodes**: Regional Spanish and Italian hubs.
## Tier 3: Institutional Authorities
* **Archival Hubs**: National Archives for over 100 sovereign nations.
* **Systemic Aggregators**: Banrepcultura, Smithsonian, DPLA, Europeana, Sudoc.
